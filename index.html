How to use
  1. Filter by Houses or Units, then sort by growth, yield, price, or crime.
  2. Tap a card to expand notes and days-to-lease.
  3. Use Domain / REA to open listings (price-capped, type-matched).
  4. Optional: tick up to 3 suburbs to compare side-by-side.

Yield = median weekly rent × 52 ÷ median price (gross). Rent is matched to dwelling type (typ. 2-bed units / 3-bed houses). Crime badges vs state baselines — verify street-level.
