AU Suburb Screen

Nationwide open-data screen · growth · yield · cashflow

How to use
  1. Set your budget, deposit, and rate in Your deal (rate defaults to RBA investor average — override anytime).
  2. List filters to suburbs whose median fits your budget. Sort by cashflow, yield, growth, price, or crime.
  3. Each card shows rent vs mortgage and net $/wk after loan costs.
  4. Tap Domain / REA for listings capped at your budget.

Coverage is partial — open government + PropTrack published tables, not every AU suburb. Use Refresh data to pull the latest published pack. Yield = rent × 52 ÷ median (gross). Cashflow: 30yr P&I on (purchase − deposit). Not financial advice.
